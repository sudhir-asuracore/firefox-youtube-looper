# Privacy Policy for YouTube Section Looper

Last updated: May 17, 2026

YouTube Section Looper (the "Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use the Extension.

## 1. No Data Collection
The Extension **does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or browsing history** to any external servers. 

## 2. Local Storage
The Extension uses your browser's local storage (`storage` permission) to save your loop configurations for specific videos. This data includes:
- Start and end timestamps for loops.
- Repeat counts.
- Playback speed and pitch settings.
- Enabled/disabled state of the looper for a specific video.

This information is stored **locally on your device** and is never uploaded to any cloud or shared with third parties.

## 3. Permissions
The Extension requires the following permissions to function:
- **`storage`**: To save and retrieve your loop settings locally.
- **`bookmarks`**: To allow you to create bookmarks for your configured loops (only used when you click the "Bookmark" button).
- **`https://www.youtube.com/*`**: To inject the user interface and control the video player on YouTube pages.

## 4. Third-Party Services
- **YouTube**: The Extension interacts with the YouTube website to provide its core functionality. We do not share any data with YouTube beyond what is necessary for the Extension to function on their site.
- **Ko-fi**: The Extension contains a link to Ko-fi for voluntary support. If you click this link, you will be directed to Ko-fi's website, which has its own privacy policy.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this document.

## 6. Contact
If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/sudhir-asuracore/firefox-youtube-looper) or contact us at `support-ytlooper@sudhirnakka.com`.
